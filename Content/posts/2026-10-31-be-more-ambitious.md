# Be More Ambitious

You have probably already heard either me, or countless other people on the internet say by now to “be more ambitious.” But, is this another herd mentality take on the internet, or does it actually have some merit?

I am one of the laziest people I know. I don’t really want much in life. I would be so much happier just rotting on the couch and watching some TV. Okay, I am joking about that (I think).
