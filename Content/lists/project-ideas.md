# Project Ideas

These projects are listed in no particular order. The idea is to just have a collection of ideas that I would love to see be shipped. I do not care if they are completed by me or not.

- [ ] Virtual wardrobe: take pictures of your clothes, use Fastion MNIST or something similar to categorize it, and generate outfit suggestions based on the user's preferences. Track outfits over time, and recommend what to add to the collection.
- [ ] Guitar Hero but for the Harmonica: Pick a song -> automagically generate a harmonica tab and then use the microphone to play.
- [ ] Empty Screenings (for Cinemark): Inspired by Riley Walz's [tweet.](https://x.com/rtwlz/status/2051331549149425728)
- [ ] AI MITM2Swagger: Capture network traffic and then use LLMs to convert it to a proper Swagger/OpenAPI specification.
- [ ] ShowHN Graveyard: Scrape links from ShowHN and see how many are dead, and how many are still alive.
- [ ] Line by Line Poster Generator: Programmatically generate [line by line art.](https://www.linebylineposters.com)
- [ ] eSCL/eSCN/AirScan app
- [ ] Unbiased Facts based news aggregator: Virtual anchor/paper which is tailored to you, and shows unbiased facts. Kind of like a virtual news agency.
- [ ] On-device content filtering: Block tweets/pictures/videos based on what you don't want to see.
